# Executive Summary: Olist Marketplace Performance (Jan 2017 – Aug 2018)

**Prepared by:** Adhiraj Sinha · **Data:** ~98k valid orders, 95k customers, 3k sellers · **Dashboard:** [live link](https://olist-analytics-adhiraj.streamlit.app/)

## Bottom line
Olist grew fast in 2017, but **growth has stalled in 2018**. Growth came only from acquiring new one-time customers, and **late deliveries are damaging both satisfaction and repeat business**. The biggest levers are delivery reliability, basket size and targeted retention of high-value customers.

## What we found
| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| Revenue | R$ 15.7M total; Jan–Aug revenue **+140% YoY**, but flat at ~R$ 1.0–1.15M/month throughout 2018 |
| Basket | Orders grew ~8×; **average order value flat at ~R$ 160** |
| Customers | ~97% buy once; **true repeat rate ≈ 2%** (after removing same-day split baskets); under 1% monthly retention in every cohort |
| Segments | "New" + "at-risk" big spenders = ~30% of customers, **~56% of revenue** |
| Delivery | 6.8% late; **late orders average 2.3★ vs 4.3★**; a late first delivery cuts repeat rate by ~19% |
| Logistics | Carrier leg ≈ 75% of delivery time; late rate hit 12–19% after demand peaks; worst in the North-East and Rio de Janeiro |
| Sellers | Top 10% of sellers = ~2/3 of revenue; 50 established sellers underperform (incl. the #2 seller) |

## Recommendations (in priority order)
1. **Delivery reliability.** Plan carrier capacity before peaks and fix routes to the North-East and RJ. *Expected impact: fewer 1–2★ reviews and more repeat purchases.*
2. **Basket size.** Push interest-free installments on high-value items (7+ installment orders ≈ 3× AOV), bundles and free-shipping thresholds.
3. **Targeted retention.** Win-back campaign for ~14k at-risk big spenders; post-purchase journeys at 60–90 days; prioritise home & fashion buyers.
4. **Seller SLAs.** Handling-time target (e.g. ship within 2 days); improvement plans for underperformers, starting with the highest-revenue ones.

## Proposed KPIs to track
True repeat rate (excluding same-day orders) · late delivery rate by state · average order value · % revenue from underperforming sellers

## Caveats
Public, anonymised data ending Aug 2018. Relationships found (e.g. lateness → lower repeat rate) are statistically significant **associations**; an A/B test would be needed to prove causation.
